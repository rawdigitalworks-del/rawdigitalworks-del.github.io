HR round

Top 15 HR interview questions and answers (2026)

Updated November 2026 · Raw Digital Works

HR rounds check your communication, attitude and fit — not just your skills. Here are the 15 questions asked most often in Indian interviews, with what the interviewer is really checking and how to answer.

1. Tell me about yourself.

Use present → past → future in about 60 seconds, with two proofs from your projects or work.

2. Why do you want to join our company?

Mention one specific thing you researched — a product, client domain, training programme or value — and link it to your goal.

3. Why should we hire you?

Match three requirements from the job description to three proofs from your background, then add one attitude point.

4. What are your strengths?

Pick two strengths that matter for the job and prove each with a short example.

5. What is your biggest weakness?

Name a real but non-critical weakness and the steps you are taking to improve it.

6. Where do you see yourself in five years?

Show ambition inside the company: a stronger specialist with more responsibility, possibly guiding new team members.

7. Why are you leaving your current job?

Stay positive: growth, new skills or bigger responsibility. Never criticise your current employer.

8. What are your salary expectations?

Research the market range for your role and city, give a range, and show flexibility for the right opportunity.

9. Are you willing to relocate?

Answer honestly. If you are limited, name the locations you can accept and why.

10. Why is there a gap in your resume?

Be brief and honest, then show what you did during the gap and that you are fully ready now.

11. How do you handle pressure?

Describe your method (break work down, prioritise) and give a real example.

12. Are you a team player?

Yes — and prove it with an example where you helped the team succeed.

13. What do you know about our company?

What they do, key products or clients, size and one recent news item — then why it interests you.

14. What is your notice period?

Give the exact period and any flexibility you can negotiate.

15. Do you have any questions for us?

Always ask two: "What does success look like in the first 90 days?" and "What does a typical day in this team look like?"
