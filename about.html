About

Built on evidence, not promises.

RAW DIGITAL WORKS is an independent studio from Nellore, India, building MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisors as digital products.

The trading-robot market is full of screenshots and promises. We do the opposite: every EA we sell comes with a written strategy, labelled test results, a version history and an honest list of its limitations. If an EA does not survive our testing, we do not sell it.

Customer questions and bug reports go straight back into improving the EAs.
