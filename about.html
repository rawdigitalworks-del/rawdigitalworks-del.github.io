About

Digital tools to grow your career and income.

Raw Digital Works is an independent digital-products studio from Nellore, Andhra Pradesh. We start with careers, and we are adding e-books, guides to making money with AI, automation tools and AI courses.

Getting a job in India often depends on small things nobody teaches: a resume the screening software can read, a clear answer to "Tell me about yourself", a LinkedIn profile recruiters can find. We turn those into simple, affordable kits written in plain English for freshers and working professionals.

Everything we sell is our own original work. We do not promise jobs — we help you present yourself clearly and prepare well.

Raw Digital Works also builds MetaTrader 5 tools for international customers.
