Validation

How we test every EA

A strategy has to survive data it was never tuned on before we sell it.

1. A precise specification

Exact indicators, entry and exit rules, stops, filters and risk per trade — specific enough that two developers would build the same EA.

2. Realistic backtests

MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester with real ticks or 1-minute OHLC data, the broker's spread, commission and swap, and an execution delay to model slippage.

3. Out-of-sample testing

We keep a later period completely unseen during development and test on it separately. Big differences between in-sample and out-of-sample results mean the EA is rejected or reworked.

4. Robustness checks

Nearby input values, other symbols and timeframes where relevant, minimum trade counts, drawdown and losing-streak analysis.

5. Demo forward testing

Running on a demo account in real time before release.

6. Release review

Legal and security checks, an installation guide, honest product claims and a final owner approval. Every published result states its type: BacktestOut-of-sampleDemo forwardLive

Testing reduces risk but cannot remove it. Markets change, and results in the future can be worse than anything seen in testing.
