ATS resume format for freshers (2026 guide)

Updated November 2026 · Raw Digital Works

Most companies in India now screen resumes with an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) before a recruiter reads them. If the software cannot read your resume, or your resume does not contain the words in the job description, you may be rejected without a human ever seeing it. The good news: an ATS-friendly resume is simple to build.

1. Use a single-column layout

Two columns, tables, text boxes, icons and photos often get scrambled when an ATS converts your file to text. Keep one column, left-aligned, with clear section headings.

2. Use standard section headings

Use headings the software expects: Summary, Education, Skills, Projects, Internship / Experience, Certifications, Achievements. Creative headings like "My Journey" can confuse the parser.

3. Order for freshers

  1. Name and contact — city, phone, professional email, LinkedIn and GitHub (for tech roles).
  2. Summary — 3 lines: degree, top skills, one proof, target role.
  3. Education — degree with CGPA, Intermediate and SSC with %/GPA.
  4. Skills — grouped (Technical, Tools, Languages).
  5. Projects — 2–3 projects, each with 2 result-focused bullets.
  6. Internship / training, then Certifications and Achievements.

4. Match keywords from the job description

Read the job post and list the skills and tools it repeats (for example SQL, Excel, Python, communication). Add the ones you genuinely have to your Summary and Skills. Never add skills you cannot discuss in an interview.

5. Write achievement bullets, not duties

Start with an action verb and add a number: "Built a Power BI dashboard that cut weekly report time from 2 hours to 10 minutes" beats "Responsible for reports".

6. Keep the file simple

  • Standard fonts (Calibri, Arial), 10–11 pt body.
  • One page for freshers.
  • Save as PDF unless the portal asks for Word, named Firstname_Lastname_Role_Resume.pdf.

Common mistakes that get resumes rejected

  • Photo, date of birth, father's name and full address — not needed for most private-sector jobs.
  • An "Objective" that talks only about what you want.
  • Spelling mistakes and an unprofessional email ID.
  • The same resume sent to every job without tailoring.

FAQ

Should I add a photo to my resume in India?

For most private-sector and IT jobs, no. Photos can break ATS parsing and are not needed unless the employer specifically asks.

Is a one-page resume enough for freshers?

Yes. Recruiters spend seconds on a first scan; one focused page with projects and skills works best for freshers.

PDF or Word for resumes?

PDF keeps formatting intact and is accepted by most portals. Use Word (.docx) only when the portal asks for it.
